My Work Process

01

Discovery

I start with a detailed questionnaire, then an optional Fit Call to confirm your goals, scope, and the right package for where you are.

02

Proposal & agreement

I confirm a fixed price, a clear timeline, and defined revision terms in writing, so there are no surprises once the work begins.

03

Strategy

I define your positioning, personality, and messaging foundation first, so every visual decision that follows is grounded in a clear point of view.

04

Concepts

I translate that strategy into initial creative direction and logo concepts, then walk you through the thinking and reasoning behind each one.

05

Refinement

We refine the direction together through structured revision rounds, defined per package, until it feels fully, unmistakably right for your brand.

06

Delivery & handoff

I hand off your final files and a complete brand guide, plus a walkthrough so your team can apply the brand consistently going forward.
