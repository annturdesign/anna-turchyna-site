HOW I WORK

My Work Process

We start with a questionnaire about your business and goals. An optional call helps us confirm the right package for you.

You get a written proposal with a fixed price, timeline, and number of revisions. No surprises once we start.

Before any design work, we define who you are and how you want to be seen. This becomes the foundation for everything visual.

I turn that strategy into creative direction and logo concepts, with an explanation of the thinking behind each option.

We refine the chosen direction together through a set number of revision rounds, until it feels exactly right.

You receive all final files and a complete brand guide, plus a walkthrough so your team knows exactly how to use it.
